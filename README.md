# HASAD — Smart Farming 

**A Data-Driven Smart Farming Project for Bahrain**

HASAD is a smart farming project developed during the NASA Space Apps Challenge Bahrain 2026. The project explores how NASA Earth observation data, local environmental information, and machine learning concepts can support agricultural decision-making in Bahrain.

Our goal was to explore a practical approach to helping farmers understand environmental conditions, compare crop options, and consider crop rotation strategies.

**Achievement: Local Impact Award — NASA Space Apps Challenge Bahrain 2026**

---

## The Challenge

Agriculture in Bahrain faces several environmental and operational challenges, including water availability, changing weather conditions, soil characteristics, and access to relevant agricultural information.

We wanted to explore how available environmental data and technology could help address some of these challenges through a smart farming concept.

## The Development Journey

HASAD was developed during an intensive two-day hackathon, from November 14 to November 15, 2026.

Within this limited timeframe, our team worked on:

- Researching the agricultural challenges relevant to Bahrain.
- Exploring available NASA and local environmental datasets.
- Investigating possible data sources for climate, weather, and geographic information.
- Designing the smart farming concept and its decision-support workflow.
- Developing an interactive prototype.
- Exploring machine learning approaches for crop ranking and soil-image classification.
- Preparing the final project presentation and demonstration.

## Data Research and Sources

An important part of the project was identifying relevant environmental and geographic data.

We explored several data resources:

| Data Source | Project Relevance |
|---|---|
| NASA POWER API | Environmental and meteorological information |
| Bahrain Open Data | Local environmental datasets, including average temperature data for 2025 |
| Bahrain Spatial Data Infrastructure (BSDI) | Geographic and spatial information |

### Data Access Challenges

Finding relevant local agricultural datasets was one of the challenges during development.

Although several data resources were available, identifying suitable datasets, understanding their availability, and determining how they could support the project required additional research.

Our team explored available local and international sources and worked with the information accessible within the hackathon timeframe.

## Project Outcome

At the end of the two-day challenge, our team delivered:

1. **Interactive Web Prototype:** A mobile-style smart farming interface presenting the HASAD concept.
2. **Environmental Data Exploration:** A Python utility for retrieving NASA POWER data.
3. **Experimental Machine Learning Components:** Initial implementations exploring crop ranking and soil-image classification.
4. **Project Presentation:** A presentation explaining the problem, proposed solution, data resources, and system workflow.

The project demonstrates how environmental information, data science, and AI concepts can be brought together to explore agricultural decision support.
